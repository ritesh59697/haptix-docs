## 1. Nature of the Service

Haptix provides non-custodial smart wallet software engineered natively for Solana Mobile and the Seeker ecosystem. It allows you to manage cryptographic assets on Solana and interact with decentralized applications, including delegating cryptographically-bounded on-chain execution authority to AI agents.

### 1.1 Non-Custodial Architecture
Haptix is fully non-custodial. We do not have access to your private keys, passkeys, biometric data, recovery phrases, or funds. Your passkeys are generated and securely stored in your device's physical hardware enclave (e.g., Android Keystore with StrongBox / Titan M2, Apple Secure Enclave, or hardware WebAuthn authenticators).

### 1.2 User Responsibility
Because Haptix is non-custodial, **you are solely responsible for securing your device and configuring recovery mechanisms.** If you lose access to your primary and backup hardware authenticators without configuring a Guardian Recovery mechanism, your assets will be permanently inaccessible. We cannot recover your funds or reset your passkeys.
